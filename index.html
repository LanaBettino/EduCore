Bursaries

NSFAS – the main one

The National Student Financial Aid Scheme funds eligible students at public universities and TVET colleges.

  • Must be a South African citizen (or eligible permanent resident as per NSFAS rules).
  • Combined household income usually up to R350,000 a year.
  • Applications usually open in the second half of the year – check the official site for this year’s dates.
  • You apply once on myNSFAS, and also apply to your institution.
